The world of Yarn

Background

  • Into Yarn is a Pretoria-based yarn and craft business serving the local knitting and crochet community. Pretoria has an involve craft culture maintained by local industries and experts in yarn traders.
    • Mission
  • Yarn intents to offers quality and affordable yarn, materials and craft supplies while supporting crafters of all levels of skills through guidance, and patterns.
    • Vision
  • The vision is to create a welcoming community centre where the community can enjoy creativity, social connection and the healing benefits of knitting and crochet.
    • Target Market
  • Knitters and crocheters, social crafters, and craft admirers. The focus on community and craft activities reflects Pretoria's established local craft culture.
