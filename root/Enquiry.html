  • Transforming raw fiber into yarn is an ancient craft that turns loose material like sheep's wool, alpaca, or cotton into a continuous usable strand for knitting and crochet. By drafting and twisting fibers together using tools like a drop spindle or spinning wheel, makers control texture, thickness, and color to create personalized yarn.The Steps to Make YarnPrep the fiber: Clean and comb raw wool or fluff so all tiny hairs face the same direction.Drafting: Pull out a small, even amount of loose fiber from your supply stack.Twisting: Add a spin twist using a tool to lock the loose fibers into a tight line.Plying: Twist two or more single strands together to make the final yarn strong and balanced. Setting the twist: Soak the finished yarn in warm water and let it dry completely
